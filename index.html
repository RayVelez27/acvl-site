The Association of Consumer Vehicle Lessors is the national association representing members that are the nation's largest consumer vehicle lessors, including captive and non-captive finance companies, banks, credit unions and leasing companies.

Our Mission

To promote high standards in vehicle leasing and to keep members well-informed on trends, changes, and key issues in the leasing industry with high expertise and integrity.

Expert Network

Includes leasing professionals who serve as board members, committee chairs and industry expert sponsors, advisors and speakers.

Focus Topics

Economic trends, used vehicle market, regulations, legal and tax matters, and industry risks.

Member Engagement

Monthly committee meetings, webinars, and in-person conferences as forums for collaboration and learning.

Industry Events

ACVL Events &
Conferences

ACVL hosts a variety of events throughout the year designed to bring together industry leaders, stakeholders, and professionals in the consumer vehicle leasing space. Our gatherings provide valuable opportunities for networking, knowledge sharing, and professional development.

From annual conferences to specialized workshops and committee meetings, ACVL events address the current challenges facing the industry including tax, operations, legal, and residual risk management. Nationally renowned speakers share insights on leasing trends and forecast changes that may impact the business. Join fellow professionals to learn the latest innovations in auto leasing and connect with colleagues from across the country.
