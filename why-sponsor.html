Why Sponsor ACVL?

Sponsorship

Partner With ACVL

ACVL offers sponsors direct access to a focused community of professionals in the consumer vehicle leasing space. Sponsorship provides year-round visibility, opportunities to share relevant expertise, and meaningful engagement through educational programming, virtual meetings, webinars and national in-person events.

Key Sponsor Benefits

Visibility

  • Recognition in ACVL communications
  • Logo placement on event and conference materials
  • Website listing & branding opportunities

Engagement

  • Access to ACVL's educational webinars and committee meetings
  • Opportunities to contribute relevant industry insights
  • Direct interaction with ACVL Members

Event Attendance

  • Spring Tax Conference: Ryan, Plano, TX — April 23rd & 24th
  • Annual Conference: Renaissance Hotel, Asheville, NC — October 11th–13th

Virtual Meetings throughout the Year

  • Residual & Operations Webinar: May 14th, 12–1:30pm
  • Monthly Tax & Legal Committee Meetings

Partner With Us

Sponsorship supports ACVL's mission of education, connection, and industry awareness.
