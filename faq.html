Frequently Asked Questions

The Association of Consumer Vehicle Lessors (ACVL) is the trade association of major retail automobile lessors who share the belief that high ethical standards of conduct and vigorous competition are the best ways to foster the growth of and public confidence in consumer vehicle leasing.

The Association's mission is to promote retail automobile leasing; eliminate unfair and deceptive trade practices; increase the effectiveness of members as competitors; and enhance the public understanding of and satisfaction with automobile leasing.

Companies interested in ACVL membership should contact us at HelloACVL@gmail.com. Membership is open to major retail automobile lessors who are committed to high ethical standards and industry best practices. Our team will provide you with information about membership requirements and benefits.

Vehicle leasing offers several advantages including lower monthly payments, the ability to drive a new car more frequently, warranty coverage for the lease term, and no concerns about trade-in value or selling the vehicle. Leasing may be ideal for those who prefer driving newer vehicles with the latest features and technology.

ACVL conferences are fully funded by our sponsors and offered at no cost to approved participants, partners, advisors, and invited speakers. Registration is required for every attendee, including speakers, to ensure access to all sessions and materials. Visit our Events section or contact us to learn about upcoming conferences.

ACVL has several committees that address key industry topics including Tax, Operations, Legal, and Residual Risk Management. These committees bring together industry experts to discuss challenges, share best practices, and develop guidance for members.

ACVL offers sponsorship opportunities at Platinum, Gold, and Silver levels. Sponsors gain visibility within the industry and support the association's mission to promote ethical vehicle leasing practices. Contact us at HelloACVL@gmail.com for sponsorship information and packages.

The ACVL 990 is available upon request. Please contact us at HelloACVL@gmail.com to request a copy of our public filings.
